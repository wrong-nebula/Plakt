The construction

The copy is set in the chosen face and fitted into the field. Its outlines become a mask: an exact winding test on the outline, and a signed distance field for soft edges and contour fields.

Every rule is sampled along its length. Each point gets an insideness t — 0 or 1 with a hard edge, a soft step, ramp, dome or terrace otherwise — and blends the outside and inside states: offset, wave, weight and visibility. Hard edges are found by bisection on the outline, where the rule is cut, jogged or ticked. Warps (row pinch, noise) bend the field before the letters do. Pens turn each run into a ribbon, dashes or dots.
